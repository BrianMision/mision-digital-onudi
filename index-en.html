The Zoom Out

From a signature to a verification system.

One signed order is evidence. A thousand signed orders are a chain of custody. A hundred SMEs with chain of custody are an auditable database for an entire sector.

In your languageThis is exactly what UNIDO calls an MRV system: you can't verify what you can't attribute to who did it, and when. We provide verifiable provenance. The environmental indicator is defined by the program.
