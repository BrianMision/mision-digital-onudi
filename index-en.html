Fluency

We speak government. Fluently.

Selling to government is a discipline of its own. We work directly with public and legal bodies, and we know their rules, their formats, their policy, and how they communicate. That fluency, not the software, is the part nobody else has.

The proof: a live API with the Flemish Energy & Climate Agency (VEKA), Belgium. Certified maintenance data moves directly between the field and a national regulator.

Their rules, their formats

We build to how a regulator actually reads: the codes, the fields, the submission formats, the policy language. Compliance is the spine of the product, not a checklist bolted on later.

B2G is its own craft

Working with government is not a feature you add at the end. We have earned that trust once already, inside one of Europe's most demanding regulatory systems.

Different markets, different rules. The fluency to speak each one is what actually travels.

The ask, and a thank-you

Introduce us to HVAC companies. We finish their last 10%.

For those companies this is dollars at a discount, not a build from scratch. The tool is done, proven, and translated. We simply finish it for them and put it to work.

To localize it we need two things, both small and specific: local device information and maintenance guidelines. Fill those two blanks and an HVAC company here is live.

So here is our one ask: open the door to the HVAC companies, or to the source that holds those two things. Device information + maintenance guidelines · the last 10%
